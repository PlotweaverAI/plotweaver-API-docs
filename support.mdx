## Send us these, and we can usually answer in one reply

1. The `X-Request-ID` of the request (or the time it was made, with your timezone).
2. The endpoint, language and voice.
3. What you expected and what you got. For audio, attach the file or say where in it the problem is.

## Response times

Pay as you go and Starter get email support. Growth adds a shared channel with same-business-day answers, Business a named engineer. See the [pricing page](https://plotweaver.app/pricing).

## Pronunciation feedback

Heard a word said wrong? Send us the text, the language and the voice. Real examples from real products are how our voices get better, and we read every one.

Follow [@plotweaverapp on X](https://x.com/plotweaverapp) and [PlotWeaver on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/plotweaver) for new voices, languages and models.
